How to Set Up Bank Rules in
QuickBooks Online
Learn how to set up bank rules in QuickBooks Online to speed up the way
you categorize bank and credit card transactions. Bank rules can pre-
categorize transactions for your review, or automatically add them to your
books, saving you time on routine bookkeeping tasks.

Why use bank rules
Bank rules in QuickBooks Online help speed up categorizing transactions.
They can pre-categorize transactions for your review or automatically add
them to your books, reducing the manual work involved in reviewing each
transaction individually.

Prerequisites
      Access to your QuickBooks Online company with bank or credit card
       accounts connected.
      Permission to access the Accounting menu and create rules.

Step 1: Navigate to the bank rules section
Select All Apps from the left navigation menu, then go to Accounting.
Hover over Accounting and select Rules from the dropdown menu to open
the Rules Center.




QuickBooks dashboard with the cursor selecting "Rules" under "Accounting" in the All
                                   Apps menu

Step 2: Create a new bank rule
Click New Rule to begin creating a rule. Enter a name for your rule in the
"What do you want to call this rule?" field — for example, Tractor rental.

Step 3: Set the rule criteria
Choose whether the rule applies to Money in or Money out transactions,
then select which bank and credit card accounts it should apply to (for
example, "All bank accounts").

Add conditions to define when the rule applies:

      Filter by Description or Bank text (the details sent by your bank).
      Specify whether the text Contains or Does not contain certain words
       — for example, "Tractor rental".
      Click + Add a condition to add more conditions if needed.

Step 4: Test the rule
Click Test rule to check whether it applies to any currently unreviewed
transactions. Review the feedback message, such as "This rule will apply to
1 currently pending transaction." If the rule doesn't behave as expected,
adjust the conditions and test again.

Step 5: Assign transaction details
Decide what QuickBooks should do with transactions that meet your rule
criteria:

      Select the Transaction type (for example, Expense).
      Enter the Category you want QuickBooks to apply (for example, Rent,
       Office Rent, Machine Rent, or Equipment Rental).
      Optionally, select a Product/service.
      Click + Add a split if you want to divide the transaction between
       multiple categories by percentage or amount.

Step 6: Add payee and customer information
Assigning a Payee to your rule improves record accuracy and makes
reconciliation easier. If your company settings allow it, you can also assign
a Customer or Project.

Step 7: Enable Auto-add for your rule (optional)
Locate the Automatically confirm transactions this rule applies to section.
Toggle Auto-add to ON if you want QuickBooks to automatically add
matching transactions to your books without review.

Step 8: Save the rule
After entering all required information and confirming the rule works as
intended, click Save to activate it.

Following these steps lets you automate and streamline how you categorize
bank and credit card transactions in QuickBooks Online.

Managing existing bank rules
Edit, copy, disable, or delete a rule
      Click Edit in the Actions column to make changes to an existing rule.
      Select Disable from the Actions dropdown to temporarily stop a rule
       from applying.
      Select Delete from the Actions dropdown to permanently remove a
       rule.

Change rule priority
QuickBooks applies rules in the order they appear in the Rules Center. To
change a rule's priority, click and drag the grid icon next to the rule and
move it up or down the list.

Export or import rules
You can export rules to use in a different QuickBooks company, or import
rules from another company if needed.

Review transactions processed by rules
Go back to Bank Transactions from the left navigation menu. Whenever
QuickBooks downloads a transaction, you can see whether a rule was
applied to it.

Select the Posted tab to view transactions that have been confirmed by you
or automatically by QuickBooks. Transactions confirmed automatically by a
rule display an AUTO label in the Transaction Posted column.

Summary
By following these steps, you can efficiently manage, automate, and review
your bank and credit card transactions in QuickBooks Online using bank
rules. This ensures accurate records, streamlined reconciliation, and
greater control over your financial data.

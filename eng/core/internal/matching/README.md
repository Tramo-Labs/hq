# matching

Links bank movements with the items they settle (invoices, expected
payments, ERP open items) and says why. Deterministic: same input, same
output, in any order. Every match carries its rule and rule version. Two
equally good candidates are never a guess: the movement waits with all of
them for a person.

## Passes, in order

| # | Rule | Links when | Tray |
|---|------|-----------|------|
| 1 | `reference_exact@v1` | a reference of the item (invoice number, cheque, Mercado Pago id, order) is a token of the movement, and the amounts are equal. No date window: an item from a previous period still matches | Auto |
| 2 | `amount_date_window@v1` | equal amount, within ±5 days of the item's due date (or date) | Auto |
| 3 | `net_of_documented_fees@v1` | the movement's own source documents a gross equal to the item and fees that explain the net (Mercado Pago). Adjustments are those fees | Suggested |
| 4 | `amount_tolerance@v1` | difference up to $10, same sign, within the window or by reference. One `rounding` adjustment | Suggested |
| 5 | `one_movement_many_items@v1` | the only subset of 2 to 6 items adding up to the movement, within the window, over at most 18 candidates. When the movement names a counterparty, the search runs over that counterparty's items | Suggested |
| 6 | `many_movements_one_item@v1` | the only subset of 2 to 6 movements adding up to the item | Suggested |

A one to one pass accepts a link only if both ends have exactly one
candidate in that pass. If either end has more, both are frozen: weaker
passes do not get to resolve an ambiguity a stronger pass found. Item
references need a digit and four characters to count.

Any match with an item below the confidence threshold (documents read with
low confidence, or unknown confidence) goes to Suggested, never Auto.

## Outcomes

- **Matches**, Auto or Suggested, with evidence: reference or counterparty,
  date distance, periods of movements and items and whether they differ
  (Duotach case 5), adjustments with their source.
- **Pending** (Suggested tray, an operator decides): `ambiguous`,
  `combination_ambiguous`, or `reference_amount_differs` (the movement names
  an item whose amount does not close: partial payment, or a fee nobody
  documented). Each with its candidates.
- **Exceptions** (the client decides): `no_candidate`.
- **Open items**: nothing settled them yet. Not an exception.

Match IDs are a hash of rule, version and links. Running the same period
twice yields the same IDs, and with the first run's matches passed as
`Settled` the second run creates none.

## Approvals

`Book` is append only: who, role, when, on which match (with a snapshot of
it), and an idempotency key. The same decision sent twice is applied once; a
second decision on a decided match is refused (decisions are not edited);
a key reused for something else is refused. Rejected links go back as
`Input.Rejected` and no pass proposes them again.

## Measured on the synthetic suite

`testdata/suite_sintetica.json`, generated by `testdata/gen_suite.py`: 200
movements of an invented pyme, labeled by construction.

| Auto | Suggested (matched) | Pending | Exception | Agree with label | False matches |
|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|
| 121 | 38 | 24 | 17 | 200 / 200 | 0 |

Four of the pending movements (category K in the generator) have two exact
candidates and a combination of the same customer's invoices that also
closes: they check that a weaker pass never settles what a stronger pass
found ambiguous. Removing that freeze turns them into 4 false matches.

**This is not an automation rate.** The movements and their labels were
written by the same author as the engine, so the suite checks that the
engine does what it was designed to do and catches regressions. The number
that goes to a client needs that client's exports labeled by a person. Bank
charges (commissions, VAT on them, the debits tax, SIRCREB) land in
exceptions today; templates for them are weeks 5 and 6.

The six Duotach acceptance cases are tests in `duotach_test.go`, each quoting
the expected result from the post; cases 4 and 6 run through the ledger.

## Endpoints, tables, jobs, events

Not wired yet; separate change on top of backend's platform. Planned:
`matching.items`, `matching.matches` (unique by match ID), `matching.pending`,
`matching.decisions` (append only by trigger), one job per tenant and period
unique by its key, and the approval endpoint behind `Idempotency-Key`.

## Sensitive data

Descriptions and counterparties of movements and items. Evidence keeps IDs,
tokens and day counts; logs keep counts and reason codes only.

## Tests

`go test ./internal/matching/`: the suite (fails on any false match or label
disagreement), shuffle determinism, rerun idempotence, the six Duotach cases
plus "reject and reprocess", confidence, duplicate reference, ambiguity not
resolved by tolerance, tolerance and window limits, combinations both ways,
rejected links blocked for every rule, and the approval book.

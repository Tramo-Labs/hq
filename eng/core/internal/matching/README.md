# matching

Links bank movements with the items they settle (invoices, expected
payments, ERP open items) and says why. Deterministic: same input, same
output, in any order. Every match carries its rule and rule version. Two
equally good candidates are never a guess: the movement waits with all of
them for a person.

## Passes, in order

| # | Rule | Links when | Tray |
|---|------|-----------|------|
| 1 | `reference_exact@v1` | a reference of the item (invoice number, cheque, Mercado Pago id, order) is a token of the movement, and the amounts are equal. No date window: an item from a previous period still matches | Auto |
| 2 | `amount_date_window@v1` | equal amount, within ±5 days of the item's due date (or date) | Auto |
| 3 | `net_of_documented_fees@v1` | the movement's own source documents a gross equal to the item and fees that explain the net (Mercado Pago). Adjustments are those fees | Suggested |
| 4 | `amount_tolerance@v1` | difference up to $10, same sign, within the window or by reference. One `rounding` adjustment | Suggested |
| 5 | `one_movement_many_items@v1` | the only subset of 2 to 6 items adding up to the movement, within the window, over at most 18 candidates. When the movement names a counterparty, the search runs over that counterparty's items | Suggested |
| 6 | `many_movements_one_item@v1` | the only subset of 2 to 6 movements adding up to the item | Suggested |

A one to one pass accepts a link only if both ends have exactly one
candidate in that pass. If either end has more, both are frozen: weaker
passes do not get to resolve an ambiguity a stronger pass found. Item
references need a digit and four characters to count.

Any match with an item below the confidence threshold (documents read with
low confidence, or unknown confidence) goes to Suggested, never Auto.

## Outcomes

- **Matches**, Auto or Suggested, with evidence: reference or counterparty,
  date distance, periods of movements and items and whether they differ
  (Duotach case 5), adjustments with their source.
- **Pending** (Suggested tray, an operator decides): `ambiguous` (two or
  more equally good candidates), `shared_candidate` (one candidate, which
  is also the only one of another movement: two equal transfers and one
  invoice; who takes it is not guessed), `combination_ambiguous`, or
  `reference_amount_differs` (the movement names an item whose amount does
  not close: partial payment, or a fee nobody documented). Each with its
  candidates.
- **Exceptions** (the client decides): `no_candidate`.
- **Open items**: nothing settled them yet. Not an exception.

Match IDs are a hash of rule, version and links. Running the same period
twice yields the same IDs, and with the first run's matches passed as
`Settled` the second run creates none.

## Approvals

`Book` is append only: who, role, when, on which match (with a snapshot of
it), and an idempotency key. The same decision sent twice is applied once; a
second decision on a decided match is refused (decisions are not edited);
a key reused for something else is refused. Rejected links go back as
`Input.Rejected` and no pass proposes them again.

## Measured on the synthetic suite

`testdata/suite_sintetica.json`, generated by `testdata/gen_suite.py`: 200
movements of an invented pyme, labeled by construction.

| Auto | Suggested (matched) | Pending | Exception | Agree with label | False matches |
|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|
| 121 | 38 | 24 | 17 | 200 / 200 | 0 |

Four of the pending movements (category K in the generator) have two exact
candidates and a combination of the same customer's invoices that also
closes: they check that a weaker pass never settles what a stronger pass
found ambiguous. Removing that freeze turns them into 4 false matches.

**This is not an automation rate.** The movements and their labels were
written by the same author as the engine, so the suite checks that the
engine does what it was designed to do and catches regressions. The number
that goes to a client needs that client's exports labeled by a person. Bank
charges (commissions, VAT on them, the debits tax, SIRCREB) land in
exceptions today; templates for them are weeks 5 and 6.

The six Duotach acceptance cases are tests in `duotach_test.go`, each quoting
the expected result from the post; cases 4 and 6 run through the ledger.

## Endpoints, tables, jobs, events

Tables (migration 00102, schema `matching`, RLS enabled and forced on all):
`items` (unique per source and source id, reloads update), `runs`,
`matches` (primary key tenant and match ID: a rerun stores nothing twice),
`pending`, and `decisions`: insert only for `tramo_app`, a trigger rejects
UPDATE and DELETE for every role, unique per idempotency key and per match
and tray.

`PGStore`, on `db.InTenant` only: `UpsertItems`, `LoadInput` (movements of
the period read from `ledger.movements`, items, settled and turned down
matches), `SaveRun` (returns how many matches are new), and `Record`.

Who decides what (product spec sections 1 and 5), enforced in `Record`:

| Tray | Who | Actions |
|------|-----|---------|
| Automatic | nobody (listed for review) | none |
| Suggested | operator | approve, reject (note required), escalate to Exceptions |
| Exceptions (escalated matches) | owner, approver | approve, reject (note required), other (note required, back to the operator) |
| Exceptions (movements with no candidate) | owner, approver | other (note required), link by hand (exact sum) |
| Suggested (movements the client marked other) | operator | resolve (note required), link by hand, return to the client (note required) |
| Any of the above | who decides that tray | revert the decision in effect |

Every note that is required (reject and other here; other, resolve and
return on movements) is checked by the server: missing or blank is 400
`note_required`. A rejection is stored with its reason (`matching.decisions`
note). No list or export shows that reason yet, neither to the operator nor
in the matches export: open item for whoever builds the operator's view.

The role is read before the idempotency key, for matches and movements:
someone whose membership decides no tray (viewer, accountant, or a role
lowered after deciding) is 403 whatever key it sends, never a replay of
another decision nor a 422 (tested with another person's key and with the
decider's own key after its role was lowered).

Nothing about the decider is input: the user is the scope's; the role is
read from `memberships` in the same transaction (a token can be ten minutes
old; a lowered role stops decisions at once); the tray is the stored one
plus any escalation, never the caller's copy of the match. One decision per
match and tray. For the next run, approved (either tray) is settled, and
rejected or "other" is turned down and not proposed again; escalated and
undecided by the client is neither. Audit actions: `matching.items_loaded`,
`matching.run`, and `matching.<action>` with the tray.

Endpoints (tag `matching`): `POST /v1/matching/runs` (owner or operator, a
month), `GET /v1/matching/matches?tray=auto|suggested|exception`,
`GET /v1/matching/pending?tray=suggested|exception` (movements of the latest
run of each month), `POST /v1/matching/matches/{matchId}/decision` (409 when
the tray already has a decision, 400 for an action the tray does not offer,
403 for a role that does not decide that tray). Every POST requires
`Idempotency-Key`.

### Movements with no candidate (migration 00103)

A movement in Exceptions with reason `no_candidate` has no match to
approve. The client (owner or approver, role read from memberships) decides
on the movement itself with `DecideMovement`
(`POST /v1/matching/movements/{movementId}/decision`):

- `other`: note required. The movement leaves the client's tray and shows
  in Suggested for the operator with reason `client_other` and the note.
- `link`: link by hand to open items whose amounts add up exactly to the
  movement. Any difference is an adjustment and stays with the operator.
  It creates a match with rule `manual_link@v1` (tray `manual`, no run)
  that is settled for the next runs while the link is in effect. Items
  already settled by a match in effect cannot be linked (`item_taken`).
- Only a movement that the latest run of its month left in Exceptions
  without a candidate can be decided (`not_in_exceptions`).

What `link` can offer comes from `GET /v1/matching/items` (`OpenItems`,
any member): the items no match in effect settles. A match is in effect
when it is automatic, approved in either tray, or a manual link in effect
(`matchInEffect`, the same rule the items guard of migration 00104
applies). A suggested match not yet approved does not settle its items.
Filters: `period` (item date in that month; without it every open item,
since an invoice of one month is often paid in the next) and `direction`
(`in` positive, `out` negative). Oldest first, up to 500 with `truncated`.

After the client's `other` the movement is the operator's (F4, migration
00106). The operator answers with:

- `resolve`: note required (what was done with it). It leaves both trays,
  also on the next runs.
- `link`: same rules as the client's link.
- `return`: note required (what the operator needs). It is back in the
  client's Exceptions with reason `operator_returned` and the operator's
  note, and the client decides again (`other` or `link`).

The operator acts only on a movement whose decision in effect is the
client's `other` (409 `not_with_operator`). Each side reverts only its own
row (403 otherwise). The decision in effect is the latest row that is not a
reversion and that nobody reverted, so reverting the operator's answer puts
the client's `other` back in effect instead of erasing it.
`open_exception_movements` counts returned movements as open.

`matching.movement_decisions` is append only (trigger), RLS forced, one
row per revision.

### Reversion

Reverting is a new recorded row with action `revert`, never an edit; the
revision numbers per match and tray (and per movement) keep the history.
For a match it undoes the latest decision in effect: the client's in
Exceptions if any, else the operator's in Suggested (reverting an
escalation sends the match back to Suggested). Only who decides that tray
may revert it. The views `matching.decision_state` and
`matching.movement_decision_state` (security invoker, so RLS applies) give
the decision in effect; everything that reads state uses them. Two
decisions at once collide on the revision: the second is 409.

Two rules for what does not exist yet:

- **Closed periods are not reverted.** The weekly close is not built. The
  check lives in one place, `PGStore.closed`, which today lets everything
  through (`Closed` is nil) and is exercised by a test with a stubbed
  close. When the close exists, it plugs in there.
- **ERP writes need compensation.** Decisions are not written to the
  client's ERP yet. When they are, reverting one that was already written
  will need a compensating entry in the ERP, never a deletion.

## Metrics

**These numbers are this tenant's, computed from this tenant's stored
data. They are not an automation rate of the product. They are not used on
the landing page or in sales until there are real statements labeled by a
person.**

`PGStore.Metrics(period, now)` computes the results panel for one month in
one tenant transaction; `GET /v1/metrics?period=YYYY-MM` serves it. Each
metric has a `source` (what it counts, over which rows, in Spanish for the
portal) and a `list` (the API path with those rows). With nothing to
measure the status is `no_data` and the value is null: no data is never
shown as zero.

| Key | Unit | What | No data when | List |
|-----|------|------|--------------|------|
| `automation_rate` | percent | movements of the month settled by an automatic match / all movements of the month | no movements, or the month was not run | matches `tray=auto&period` |
| `suggested_acceptance_rate` | percent | suggested matches of the month approved / approved plus rejected, by the decision in effect | nothing decided | matches export of the month |
| `open_exception_movements` | count | movements left in Exceptions without candidate by the latest run, with no client decision in effect | month not run | pending `tray=exception&period` |
| `open_exception_matches` | count | escalated matches of the month the client has not decided | month not run | matches `tray=exception&period` |
| `oldest_open_exception_days` | days | days since the oldest open exception movement | month not run, or none open | pending `tray=exception&period` |
| `pending_<reason>` | count | movements in Suggested by reason (ambiguous, shared_candidate, combination_ambiguous, reference_amount_differs, client_other) | month not run | pending `tray=suggested&period` |
| `invoices_processed` | count | invoices stored in the month (Argentina time) | no invoice ever stored | invoices `period` |
| `invoices_held` | count | of those, the ones not eligible for automatic | no invoice ever stored | invoices `period&autoEligible=false` |

Not in the panel because nothing stored can measure them yet: bank against
accounting difference (no ERP), duplicates caught (not stored), hours
returned (no baseline the client confirmed).

The matches export (`MatchRows`, `GET /v1/exports/matches`) has one row
per match with a movement in the date range: rule and version, tray,
links, adjustments, evidence and the decision in effect (tray, action,
user, role, when, revision). The portal writes it formula safe.

## Who sees Suggested (F20)

The client never sees the Suggested tray nor internal rules not yet
confirmed (product spec section 1); the accountant neither. Enforced in the
store, inside the tenant transaction, with the role read from memberships:
listing Suggested matches or pending movements is 403 for anyone but an
operator; the matches export gives them results only (automatic, linked by
hand, approved, and what an operator sent to Exceptions), never an
undecided or rejected suggestion; the metrics about Suggested
(`suggested_acceptance_rate`, `pending_*`) are not offered to them.
Everyone sees Automatic as a result, and Exceptions.

## Monthly job

`matching.run_month` (River, registered in `worker.New`). Arguments: the
tenant id and the period, nothing else (River stores them in clear). They
are the unique key (`ByArgs`): a month enqueued twice, or again after it
ran, runs once. `MonthWorker.Work` scopes the context with
`jobs.TenantContext` and calls `PGStore.RunMonth`, the only implementation
of a month run, which `POST /v1/matching/runs` also uses. A retry is safe:
matches are stored once by their ID. `EnqueueMonth(ctx, client, period)`
enqueues for the tenant in the scope.

Tested with a real River worker: twice and after completion, one run;
another month, another run; tenant b's run of the same month touches none
of tenant a's rows. Removing the unique key fails the first test.

Not done: who enqueues every tenant's month on a schedule. A periodic job
that lists tenants needs a cross tenant read, which matching does not do;
that fan out belongs to platform (backend).

## Sensitive data

Descriptions and counterparties of movements and items. Evidence keeps IDs,
tokens and day counts; logs keep counts and reason codes only.

## Tests

`go test ./internal/matching/`: the suite (fails on any false match or label
disagreement), shuffle determinism, rerun idempotence, the six Duotach cases
plus "reject and reprocess", confidence, duplicate reference, ambiguity not
resolved by tolerance, tolerance and window limits, combinations both ways,
rejected links blocked for every rule, and the approval book.
